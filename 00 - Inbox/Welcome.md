# 👋 Welcome

This vault template combines **Tiago Forte’s PARA method** with a **simple tagging system** for a clean, scalable workflow in Obsidian.

---

## PARA in short
**P** - Projects: short-term efforts with a clear outcome  
**A** - Areas: long-term responsibilities with no fixed end date  
**R** - Resources: reference material and knowledge that may be useful  
**A** - Archives: inactive items from the other three categories  

The PARA framework gives your vault a logical backbone.  
Learn more from [Tiago Forte](https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/), the originator of PARA.

---

## Tagging System
Alongside PARA, this template uses a **lightweight tagging workflow** inspired by [James Bedford](https://x.com/jameesy).  

Tags give you:
- Cross-cutting organisation  
- Quick filtering and searching  
- Freedom to adapt your vault without breaking structure  

---

## INBOX
All new notes, ideas, and quick captures go into the **INBOX** folder. This is your entry point — process later, don’t overthink at capture.

---

## Quick Add
With the [Quick Add](https://github.com/chhoumann/quickadd) plugin, you can instantly create:  
- **Note**  
- **Weekly Note**  
- **Monthly Note**  
- **Contact**  

Open the **Command Palette** (`cmd + P` / `ctrl + P`) and run the predefined Quick Add commands.
